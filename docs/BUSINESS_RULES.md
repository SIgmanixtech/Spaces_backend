# SIGMANIX SPACES — Business Rules Register

The single record of business rules for the application. A rule is implemented only once
its behaviour is **Confirmed** by the business owner. Statuses:

- **Proposed**: suggested by development, not yet agreed.
- **Confirmed**: agreed by the business owner; not built yet.
- **Implemented**: built; not yet fully tested end to end.
- **Tested**: built and covered by passing tests.
- **Open**: needs a business decision (see the Decisions table).

Last updated: 2 Nov 2026 (Phase 3A, ET1–ET4, 3B-1 done; 3B-2 and read-only Accounts built, not yet tested).
